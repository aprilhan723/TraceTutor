# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TraceTutor contributors, maintainers, and users commit to a respectful, harassment-free community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Protect learner privacy and use fictional examples.
- Give credit for work and disclose material AI assistance.
- Accept maintainer decisions and review feedback in good faith.
- Step away from a discussion that is becoming unproductive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Insults, trolling, intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing another person's private information
- Posting learner records, credentials, private links, or restricted assessment content
- Knowingly misrepresenting tests, adoption, learning efficacy, authorship, or security evidence
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when needed to protect the community.

For a confidential conduct report, use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and state that the report concerns community conduct. Do not place personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed for context, impact, and safety. The maintainer will avoid publicizing the identity of a reporter unless disclosure is required for safety or law.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents TraceTutor elsewhere.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
