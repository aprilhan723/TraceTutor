# Maintainer Tutor Usability Review

**Review date:** 2026-08-15  
**Build reviewed:** hosted fictional Demo Mode before the first-use clarity update  
**Reviewer context:** the primary maintainer currently tutors students and reviewed the product from both the learner and tutor perspectives

## Method

The reviewer used only the fictional Demo Mode. No learner name, answer, account, class record, contact information, or licensed lesson material was collected for this review.

The full learner correction took approximately seven to eight minutes. The reviewer then inspected the tutor diagnosis and note-taking workflow.

## Observations

### Learner flow

- The full correction could be completed without confusion.
- The learner experience felt clean once practice began.
- The main action for beginning the first problem was not prominent enough on the Today page.

### Tutor flow

- The diagnosis controls, alternate cause, next practice, follow-up question, lesson action, and student note appeared as one long undifferentiated form.
- The note field did not make its audience or expected content obvious.
- A first-time tutor could not quickly tell which action was required and which actions were optional.

### Terminology and scope

- Internal cadence abbreviations such as `D2` and `D7` did not immediately communicate “review after 2 days” and “review after 7 days.”
- The correction loop could be useful beyond TOEFL Reading, but subject expansion needs an explicit content and scoring boundary before implementation.

## Accepted changes

- Put a high-visibility start/resume action immediately after the learner page heading.
- Organize tutor adjudication into three short steps: confirm the main mistake, choose the next practice, and leave one clear note.
- Move alternate causes and unresolved-case controls behind clearly labeled optional sections.
- Explain the student note with its audience, expected length, and an original example.
- Replace cadence abbreviations in learner- and tutor-facing copy with plain-language 2-day and 7-day review labels while preserving the typed internal values.
- Add a subject-adapter design step to the roadmap instead of claiming unsupported multi-subject compatibility.

## Evidence limit

This is a domain-informed maintainer review, not independent adoption, an external tutor pilot, or evidence of learning efficacy. External, permissioned pilot use remains necessary before broader usage claims.
